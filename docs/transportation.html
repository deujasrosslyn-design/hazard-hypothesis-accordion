Transportation and response data

What it helps answer
Transportation data can show whether communities have several ways to evacuate or depend on only one road, bridge, or trail.
Hypothesis
H3 — Hypothesis 3: Transportation Isolation
Benefit
This directly supports H3 by helping identify communities that may become isolated.
Limitation
Existing maps may not include informal trails, seasonal paths, or recent damage.
What it helps answer
Road closure reports can show whether roads and bridges are still usable after flooding or landslides.
Benefit
These reports can provide current information that may not appear in older transportation datasets.
Limitation
The reports may be delayed, incomplete, or more common in areas with better communication.
What it helps answer
Shelter and emergency facility locations can help determine whether communities have realistic access to assistance during an evacuation.
Benefit
The team can measure the distance and travel access to available support.
Limitation
A facility may be mapped but unavailable, damaged, overcrowded, or difficult to reach.

These sources can show where assistance is already available and where communities may be far from existing support.
Benefit
They can help identify gaps in response coverage and resource allocation.
