Population and settlement data

What it helps answer

Settlement data can show where communities are located in relation to hazards and evacuation routes.

Benefit

It connects the hazard data to specific communities.

Limitation

Some settlements may be missing, or the data may not show recent population movement.

What it helps answer

WorldPop or LandScan could help estimate how many people may be exposed to flooding, landslides, or transportation isolation.

Benefit

It can help identify where more people may need assistance.

Limitation

The estimates may be too general or outdated, especially after people have moved because of a disaster.

What it helps answer

Information about children, older adults, people with disabilities, and displaced populations could help identify communities that may need additional support during an evacuation.

Benefit

It shows that evacuation difficulty is not only about the number of people. It also depends on the needs of the population.

Limitation

This information may be incomplete or difficult to collect consistently.

What it helps answer

Population displacement data can help show whether people have moved because of flooding, landslides, conflict, or damaged infrastructure. This is important because older population estimates may not represent where people are located during or after a disaster.

Benefit

It can provide a more current idea of who may need evacuation support.

Limitation

Displaced populations can move again, and the available information may not include people who are staying with family, using informal shelters, or living in areas that are difficult to reach.
